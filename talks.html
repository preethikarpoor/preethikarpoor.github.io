Recalibrating Chandrayaan-2 CLASS X-ray Fluorescence Spectra with Machine Learning
Contributed talk at the 5th Planetary Data Workshop and 2nd Planetary Science Informatics & Data Analytics (PSIDA) meeting. I present the application of the SymAE symmetric autoencoder to redatum XRF spectra from ISRO's Chandrayaan-2 CLASS instrument, enabling large-scale solar-state corrections and lunar surface composition mapping.
