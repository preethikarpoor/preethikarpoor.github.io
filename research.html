Exoplanets

Transit Photometry
Watching stars dim as planets cross their face. My EXOSCAPER pipeline does this at scale for M dwarfs in TESS data — hunting Earths around the galaxy's most common stars.

Direct Imaging

Planetary Science · IISc–ISRO / Caltech
Chandrayaan-2 & Lunar Surface Mapping
I adapted the SymAE autoencoder to recalibrate X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectral data from Chandrayaan-2's CLASS instrument — India's second lunar mission. The goal: better maps of elemental composition on the lunar surface by removing instrumental artifacts that confounded spectral interpretation. A genuinely unusual intersection of deep learning and planetary science, working with data from ISRO's own spacecraft.
🔩
Instrumentation · Indian Institute of Astrophysics
Telescope Design at IIA — NLST & NLOT
Before I was a data scientist, I was a mechanical engineer building actual telescope hardware. As an intern at IIA, I designed and tested components for two major Indian telescope projects: the National Large Solar Telescope (NLST) and the National Large Optical Telescope (NLOT). This included a Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor, a Faint Object Spectrograph and Camera, a Grism Wheel Assembly, and retractable dome systems. Zemax optical modeling, SolidWorks CAD, the whole deal.
